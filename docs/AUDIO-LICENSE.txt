Retro website sound-deck samples

These four MP3s are bundled soundtrack choices for the Retro website preview. They are separate from Retro's code, interface, artwork, and other project materials.

1. Lo-fi — “Cue” by TAD
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/lofi-compilation
License listed on the source page: CC0 1.0 Universal. Attribution is not required; the website credits TAD.

2. Jazz — “One Step at a Time” by Alex McCulloch (Pro Sensory)
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/one-step-at-a-time
License listed on the source page: CC0. The creator says attribution is appreciated but not required; the website credits Alex McCulloch.

3. Piano — “Piano” by maggie.beans3696
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/piano-0
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).
Attribution: “Piano” by maggie.beans3696, OpenGameArt.

4. Rainy Cafe — rain by Matt Barnard and cafe by Samuel Justice
Source and credits: https://rainycafe.com/
The Rainy Cafe page credits both recordings as Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), from SoundCloud. Rain and cafe recordings are mixed into one MP3.

These statements reflect the corresponding source pages as checked for this release; consult each source page for its full license terms and attribution context.
