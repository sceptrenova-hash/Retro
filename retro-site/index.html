05 / THE DETAILS

Quiet by default.
Capable when invited.

Retro keeps notes and session history on your Mac. Extra controls are optional and explain what macOS access they need before you enable them.

01

Your notes stay local

Focus notes, preferences, and session history are saved locally by the app.

↗
02

Permissions stay yours

Music controls, browser shields, and Do Not Disturb integrations are opt-in.

↗
03

System blocking is explicit

The optional system block requires macOS administrator authentication; it is never on by default.
